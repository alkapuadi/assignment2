Fall Harvest Festival

Saturday, November 14, 2026 · 4:00–9:00 PM · Founders Quad

Celebrate the end of the fall season with Northbridge State's biggest campus tradition. The Harvest Festival brings together student organizations, local farms, and food vendors for an evening of music, games, and community.

About the Event

Now in its 35th year, the Harvest Festival is hosted by the Student Activities Board. More than 30 student organizations run booths with games, crafts, and fundraisers, while local farms sell apple cider and fresh produce. The night ends with a lantern walk around the quad.

What to Expect

  • Live sets from three student bands on the main stage
  • Pumpkin carving and painting stations (supplies provided)
  • Food trucks with vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free options
  • A free hot cider tent for the first 500 guests
  • Lantern walk at 8:30 PM

Schedule

  1. 4:00 PM Gates open and booths begin
  2. 5:00 PM Pumpkin carving contest starts
  3. 6:00 PM Main stage live music
  4. 7:30 PM Carving contest winners announced
  5. 8:30 PM Lantern walk around Founders Quad
  6. 9:00 PM Festival closes

Accessibility & Participation

Founders Quad has paved, step-free paths to every booth, and accessible restrooms are located in Founders Hall. ASL interpretation is provided for main stage performances. A quiet tent with seating is available near the north entrance for anyone who needs a break from the crowd.

To request additional accommodations, email the Office of Student Engagement at least five days before the event.
