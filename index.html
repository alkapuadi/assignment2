About Campus Pulse

Campus Pulse is run by the Northbridge State Office of Student Engagement. We work with more than 200 registered student organizations and campus departments to publish their events in one easy-to-browse guide.

Whether you are a first-year student looking for community or a senior squeezing in one last festival, our goal is to make getting involved simple.

  • A single calendar of verified campus events
  • Free event promotion for registered student organizations
  • Accessibility details listed for every event
  • Room booking and event-planning advice from our staff
